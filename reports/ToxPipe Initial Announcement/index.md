# Abstract

This paper presents ToxPipe, an AI-assisted toxicological information retrieval and interpretation platform. ToxPipe is designed to help toxicologists and researchers quickly and accurately retrieve and interpret toxicological information from a variety of sources.

Toxicological research is an important field that plays a critical role in protecting public health and the environment. ToxPipe is designed to address this challenge by providing an AI-assisted platform that can help researchers quickly and accurately retrieve and interpret toxicological information.
